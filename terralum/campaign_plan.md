# Terralum campaign expansion plan

The seven-act cadence follows the broad chapter structure of *Shadows of Amn*. Terralum's plot and places come from the early Alsperia transcript, the later recovered lore codex, or explicitly proposed adaptation material. The current game implements a short chapter spine. The targets below describe a longer future game; they are not current playtime claims.

The current build has twenty playable scene definitions and 50 story beats across the seven-act spine. Act I includes the Royal Dispatch Archive and Royal Stables; Act II includes the Hunter's Camp, River Crossing, and Three-Cairn Fork before the road and watchpost. Act VI now includes Archivist Nera’s optional Index Warden inquiry: leave the folio sealed or defeat the Warden to recover a comparison leaf, with both paths remembered in Index Hall. Act VII adds the Expedition Chart Room, where Algoreth chooses whether to carry the marked archive copy or Selene's witness notes; the choice changes the hangar dialogue and is journaled. These are adaptation scenes and do not settle what another Tear means. The current area art and NPCs are present in the pack, but all areas still inherit demo WED geometry and character silhouettes. Eleven scenes have bespoke searchmaps; nine still use the generic demo searchmap. Algoreth answers Dorian's purpose question before the guardian fight, and the answer changes the Guardian's response and persists in the journal. The Seal Gallery requires an inscription answer, journals it, and changes Alisande's return-court response.

The intended full campaign could grow to about 25-30 quests and 20-30 distinct areas, with branching dialogue, companion arcs, meaningful combat and a later Eldrador continuation. A 30-45 hour playtime is only a design target. The current twenty-area slice still needs area-specific WED/terrain data, deeper companion arcs, authored quest journals, durable multi-stage dialogue, enemy art and balance, party systems, and save/load regression coverage.

## Lore boundary

The early Edmon–Alisande marriage is a direct user clarification. The later Algoreth–Alisande marriage and co-rule are stated in the recovered codex. Their transition, Edmon's fate, the Eldrador Tear's outcome, and the intelligence behind the Tears are unresolved in the available sources. New quests and dialogue must be marked as adaptation proposals, as described in [`lore_notes.md`](lore_notes.md).
