# Terralum campaign expansion plan

The seven-act cadence follows the broad chapter structure of *Shadows of Amn*. Terralum's plot and places come from the early Alsperia transcript, the later recovered lore codex, or explicitly proposed adaptation material. The current game implements a short chapter spine. The targets below describe a longer future game; they are not current playtime claims.

| Act | Established story anchor | Proposed additional game material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I. The Royal Audience | Algoreth seeks the Byzantem scrolls; Edmon and his wife Alisande receive him | Alsperia library, market, castle stables; investigation and preparation quests |
| II. Through Mistwood | The road to Lorian passes through Mistwood | River crossing, hunter camp, watchtower; route choice and woodland encounters |
| III. Caverns of Lorian | Dorian and the scrolls are associated with Lorian | Upper cave, trap gallery, silver vault; clues, puzzles, guardian battle |
| IV. Return to Alsperia | Algoreth returns with the scrolls | Court inquiry and research quests; a transition that does not invent Edmon's fate |
| V. Eastern Academy | Later, Algoreth reigns with Alisande; Vael reports a Tear and a false Algoreth | Academy annex, containment vault, warding encounter; playable sealing sequence |
| VI. The Living Record | The Tear remains contained; Ealdrin and Alisande research other Tears | Deep Archives investigations and spectral guardian sequence, preserving uncertainty about Eternal Archives |
| VII. Eldrador Expedition | Vaelthira invites Algoreth's investigation; an Aethercraft departs | Skyport preparation, companion conversations, voyage; end at the unresolved Eldrador threshold |

The intended full campaign could grow to about 25-30 quests and 20-30 distinct areas, with branching dialogue, companion arcs, meaningful combat and a later Eldrador continuation. A 30-45 hour playtime is only a design target. Area collision maps, quest flags, branching choices, enemy art and balance, party systems, and save/load regression coverage remain engineering work.

## Lore boundary

The early Edmon–Alisande marriage is a direct user clarification. The later Algoreth–Alisande marriage and co-rule are stated in the recovered codex. Their transition, Edmon's fate, the Eldrador Tear's outcome, and the intelligence behind the Tears are unresolved in the available sources. New quests and dialogue must be marked as adaptation proposals, as described in [`lore_notes.md`](lore_notes.md).
