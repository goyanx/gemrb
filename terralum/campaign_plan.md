# Terralum campaign expansion plan

The seven-act cadence follows the broad chapter structure of *Shadows of Amn*. Terralum's plot and places come from the early Alsperia transcript, the later recovered lore codex, or explicitly proposed adaptation material. The current game implements a short chapter spine. The targets below describe a longer future game; they are not current playtime claims.

The current build has sixteen playable scene definitions across the seven-act spine. Act II now includes a Mistwood Hunter's Camp and the Three-Cairn Fork before the road and watchpost; Acts I–V have at least two locations, and Acts VI–VII have two each. Deeper quest arcs remain. The current area art and NPCs are present in the pack, but all areas still inherit the demo WED geometry and character silhouettes. The camp, fork, road, watchpost, and Lorian Cavern have bespoke searchmaps; eleven areas still use the generic demo searchmap. Algoreth now has to answer Dorian's purpose question before the guardian fight, and the answer changes the Guardian's response and persists in the journal.

| Act | Established story anchor | Current scene areas | Next expansion target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I. The Royal Audience | Algoreth seeks the Byzantem scrolls; Edmon and his wife Alisande receive him | Alsperia Market; Alsperia Library | Stables and a court archive task; add a clue and journal entry |

| V. Eastern Academy | Later, Algoreth reigns with Alisande; Vael reports a Tear and a false Algoreth | Academy Courtyard; Observatory Vault | Containment annex and warding challenge; make the sealing event playable |
| VI. The Living Record | The Tear remains contained; Ealdrin and Alisande research other Tears | Deep Archives; Index Hall | Add a scholar's quest and guardian sequence; preserve uncertainty about Eternal Archives |
| VII. Eldrador Expedition | Vaelthira invites Algoreth's investigation; an Aethercraft departs | Alsperia Skyport; Aethercraft Hangar | Add companion preparation scenes; end at the unresolved Eldrador threshold |

The intended full campaign could grow to about 25-30 quests and 20-30 distinct areas, with branching dialogue, companion arcs, meaningful combat and a later Eldrador continuation. A 30-45 hour playtime is only a design target. The current sixteen-area slice still needs area-specific WED/terrain data, authored quest journals, more durable dialogue branches, enemy art and balance, party systems, and save/load regression coverage.

## Lore boundary

The early Edmon–Alisande marriage is a direct user clarification. The later Algoreth–Alisande marriage and co-rule are stated in the recovered codex. Their transition, Edmon's fate, the Eldrador Tear's outcome, and the intelligence behind the Tears are unresolved in the available sources. New quests and dialogue must be marked as adaptation proposals, as described in [`lore_notes.md`](lore_notes.md).
