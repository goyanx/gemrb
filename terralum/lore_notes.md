# Terralum lore notes for the game

This is a working adaptation note, not a replacement for the user's source files. The early scroll quest is supported by the local GolemDungeon Alsperia transcript; the later Tear arc is supported by the user-supplied `TERRALUM_LORE_CODEX.md` handoff of 2026-11-09. The codex is a partial reconstruction from recaps, so its uncertainties remain uncertainties here. Neither source is copied into the public game pack.

## Established material used in the current campaign

- Algoreth Jon Goren begins the game as a powerful wizard seeking the scrolls of Byzantem, which concern world travelling. The early transcript places him in Alsperia, sends him through Mistwood to the Caverns of Lorian, and names Dorian the Seer as the scrolls' last keeper.
- Edmon is the original King of Alsperia in this story. At his early court, Alisande is **Edmon's wife and queen**. The user clarified both his place in the royal chronology and their marriage directly on 2026-11-09.
- In the later recovered chronicle, Algoreth is Wizard King and **Alisande is his wife and co-ruler**. The available material does not establish when or how Edmon's reign or marriage ended, or how Algoreth and Alisande married. The game therefore skips that missing interval rather than inventing Edmon's fate.
- Later, Magister Vael reports a disturbance beneath the eastern magical academy. Algoreth meets a false version of himself and stabilizes the Tear of Chaos by imposing a law of form and purpose. The false self remains contained.
- Ealdrin and Alisande join research into other Tears. A recovered Deep Archives account associates them with extraordinary power confronting its limits. Whether Deep and Eternal Archives are the same place is unresolved.
- Queen Vaelthira of Eldrador faces another Tear. Algoreth, Alisande, Seraphina and Ealdrin prepare to investigate; an Aethercraft departure appears in recovered accounts. The Eldrador outcome is unknown.

## New game material and limits

Archivist Mara, Scout Tessa, their dialogue, the waystone inscription, the Lorian guardian encounter, and the vault echo are **proposed adaptation content**. The first source names Dorian and Lorian but does not provide all the scripted dialogue used here. Combat uses provisional demo creature art. No game scene implies a verified resolution of Eldrador's Tear, the false Algoreth's future, the Archives' exact relationship, or Edmon's fate.

The children Elara, Lyriana and Alric, Lady Elowen, Tidewarden Thalor and the spectral guardians belong to the wider recovered lore but are not yet staged in the playable prototype. Their details and chronology need careful source review before use. Golemnus is a Dungeon Master in the source context, not an established in-world NPC. Synthia and Demerzel belong to separate stories.
