# Area art prompts

The backgrounds were generated with the built-in image generator for this project, then copied here unchanged. The builder crops and resizes each source to GemRB's 25 × 18 tile layout.

Shared direction: wide 1600 × 1152 playable isometric fantasy RPG area; hand-painted late 1990s pre-rendered environment style; clear walkable central floor; no characters, text, UI, or watermark; original setting and design.

| `alsperia_court.png` | Royal throne hall, two restrained thrones, scholars' tables, warm afternoon light. |
